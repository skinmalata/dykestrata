1. Who we are

This website is operated by DYKESTRATA SERVICES LIMITED, a private company limited by shares incorporated in the Federal Republic of Nigeria (RC 9931890). For the purposes of applicable data protection law, the company acts as the data controller for personal information collected through this website.

2. Information we collect

We only collect information that is necessary to respond to enquiries. This may include:

  • Your name.
  • Your email address.
  • Your telephone number, if you choose to provide it.
  • The name of your company or organisation, if provided.
  • The subject and content of your message.
  • Basic technical information necessary to operate and secure the website.

3. How we use information

  • To respond to your enquiry and correspond with you.
  • To prepare a quotation or proposal where you have asked for one.
  • To maintain records of business communications.
  • To protect the website against misuse, spam, and abuse.

We do not sell personal information.

4. Legal basis

Where you submit an enquiry, we process your information on the basis of your consent and our legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries. You may withdraw consent at any time by contacting us, though this may prevent us from responding.

5. Storage and security

Information submitted through the contact form is transmitted over HTTPS and handled by the service providers described below. We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures appropriate to the sensitivity of the information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Service providers and sharing

We may use trusted third-party providers (for example, hosting and email delivery) to operate this website. Where we do, they process information only as necessary to provide their service to us. A current list of processors should be documented here before launch.

7. Retention and deletion

We retain enquiry information only for as long as necessary to deal with the enquiry and for a reasonable period afterwards for record keeping, after which it is deleted or anonymised. The specific retention period should be confirmed before publication.

8. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to lodge a complaint with the relevant authority. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

9. Cookies and analytics

This website does not currently set advertising cookies. If privacy-conscious analytics are enabled, this section will be updated to name the provider, describe what is measured, and explain how to opt out. No analytics are loaded unless configured by the site owner.

10. International transfers

Some service providers may store or process information outside Nigeria. Where this occurs, the company will take reasonable steps to ensure appropriate safeguards are in place.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above will be revised accordingly.
