Who We Are

A Nigerian company built on dependable service.

DYKESTRATA SERVICES LIMITED is a private company limited by shares, incorporated in the Federal Republic of Nigeria under the Companies and Allied Matters Act 2020. Our registration number is RC 9931890.

We provide subsurface services to operators and asset owners — covering seismic, field development and prospectivity studies, petrophysics, and pore pressure and geomechanics — working from the data you already have to the decisions you need to make.

Our Services

Subsurface services that turn data into decisions.

Four connected disciplines covering the subsurface life of a field — from the seismic image, to the rock and fluid properties, to the plan for developing the resource safely and economically.

Seismic Acquisition, Processing & Interpretation

Survey design, data QC, 2D/3D processing and full structural and stratigraphic interpretation to build a trustworthy subsurface image.

Learn more

Field Development & Prospectivity Studies

Lead and prospect ranking, volumetrics and development concepts that guide optimal exploitation of the resource base.

Petrophysics

Log, core and test evaluation covering porosity, permeability, saturation, net pay, fluid contacts and reservoir zonation.
