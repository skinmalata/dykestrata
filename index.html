Who We Are

A Nigerian company built on dependable service.

DYKESTRATA SERVICES LIMITED is a private company limited by shares, incorporated in the Federal Republic of Nigeria under the Companies and Allied Matters Act 2020. Our registration number is RC 9931890.

We provide subsurface, environmental and oil and gas services to operators and asset owners — covering seismic, field development and prospectivity studies, petrophysics, and pore pressure and geomechanics, with environmental services and facilities and project support around them — working from the data you already have to the decisions you need to make.

Our Services

Subsurface services that turn data into decisions.

Six connected service lines covering the life of a field — from the seismic image, to the rock and fluid properties, to the plan for developing the resource safely and economically, with the environmental, facilities and project support around it.

Seismic Acquisition, Processing & Interpretation

Survey design, data QC, 2D/3D processing and full structural and stratigraphic interpretation to build a trustworthy subsurface image.

Field Development & Prospectivity Studies

QHSE

Quality, health, safety and environment, built into how we work.

Every study and every report is checked before it leaves us, planned around safe working, and carried out with the environment and the people doing the work in mind.

The company's formal QHSE policy statement, and any certification status, will be published here once approved.
Quality
Work reviewed before issue, with document control and traceable revisions.
Health
Safe working practices for office and field activities, with attention to the people doing the work.
Safety
Hazards identified and controlled before work starts, with incidents and near misses reported and reviewed.
Environment
Field activities planned to minimise disturbance, with waste handled and disposed of responsibly.
