Who We Are

Integrated geoscience for confident decisions.

Dykestrata Services Limited is a technical services company focused on subsurface characterisation, reservoir evaluation, geophysical interpretation, and integrated geoscience solutions. We combine technical expertise, analytical workflows, and collaborative project delivery to help clients understand geological complexity, evaluate reservoir potential, and make informed exploration and development decisions.

Our approach connects geological interpretation, geophysical data, petrophysical evaluation, and engineering insight to support the complete subsurface evaluation workflow.

Our Services

Subsurface services that turn data into decisions.

Six connected service lines supporting safe, informed development across the life of an asset.

Seismic Acquisition, Processing & Interpretation

Survey design, data QC, 2D/3D processing and full structural and stratigraphic interpretation to build a trustworthy subsurface image.

Learn more

Field Development & Prospectivity Studies

Lead and prospect ranking, volumetrics and development concepts that guide optimal exploitation of the resource base.

Learn more

Petrophysics

Log, core and test evaluation covering porosity, permeability, saturation, net pay, fluid contacts and reservoir zonation.

Learn more

Pore Pressure & Geomechanics

Pressure prediction, wellbore stability, mud weight windows and casing seat selection for safer, more predictable drilling.

Learn more

Environmental Services

Baseline and impact studies, management plans, permitting support, waste and spill planning, remediation and monitoring.

Learn more

Oil and Gas Services: Facilities & Project Support

Concept selection, FEED and design support, process and flow assurance studies, commissioning and brownfield modification support.
