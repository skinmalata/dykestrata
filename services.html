Our Services

Our core offering is a set of integrated subsurface services that take seismic and well data through to decisions on where to drill, how to develop, and how to produce safely and economically — supported by environmental services and oil and gas facilities and project support.

Service Lines

Integrated capability, from subsurface image to project delivery.

Service 01

Seismic Acquisition, Processing and Interpretation

End-to-end seismic support — from survey design and data quality control through processing to structural and stratigraphic interpretation.

The problem it addresses

Subsurface decisions are only as good as the seismic image behind them. Poor acquisition parameters, under-processed data or incomplete interpretation lead to misplaced wells, missed closures and avoidable dry-hole risk.

Proposed scope of work

Survey design and acquisition planning, QC of field data, 2D and 3D seismic processing, structural and stratigraphic interpretation, horizon and fault mapping, seismic attribute analysis, and play and prospect mapping.

Enquire about this service

Deliverables

  • Processed seismic sections and volumes
  • Interpreted horizon and fault maps
  • Structure, isopach and attribute displays
  • Prospect maps and interpretation report

Deliverables

  • Prospectivity evaluation and lead/prospect ranking
  • Volumetric estimates and risking
  • Field development concept options
  • Exploitation roadmap and recommendations
Service 02

Field Studies: Field Development & Prospectivity

Field development and prospectivity studies that support optimal exploitation of the resource base.

The problem it addresses

Assets are often developed on precedent rather than on evidence. Without a ranked view of the remaining leads and a tested development concept, capital is committed to the wrong locations and recovery is left on the table.

Proposed scope of work

Play and prospectivity evaluation, mapping and ranking of leads and prospects, volumetric estimation and risking, field development options, well spacing and phasing, recovery strategy, and production optimisation reviews.

Enquire about this service
Service 03

Petrophysics

Petrophysical evaluation of well log, core and test data that describes reservoir quality, fluids and net pay.

The problem it addresses

Inconsistent or unverified petrophysical assumptions propagate into every later calculation. Uncertain porosity, saturation and net-to-gross figures make volumes unreliable and weaken completion and facility decisions.

Proposed scope of work

Log QC and environmental corrections, porosity, permeability and water saturation evaluation, net-to-gross and pay classification, fluid contact determination, core-to-log calibration, rock typing and reservoir zonation.

Enquire about this service

Deliverables

  • Petrophysical evaluation report
  • Digitised log suite with computed curves
  • Net pay, porosity and saturation tables
  • Reservoir zonation and rock-type scheme

Deliverables

  • Pore pressure and fracture gradient profiles
  • Mud weight window and wellbore stability assessment
  • Casing seat and drilling margin recommendations
  • Geomechanical model and full report
Service 04

Pore Pressure and Geomechanics

Pore pressure prediction and geomechanical assessment that support safe, cost-effective drilling and stable well design.

The problem it addresses

Unexpected pore pressure and rock failure are among the most common causes of non-productive time — lost circulation, wellbore instability, stuck pipe and blowouts — and they are rarely cheap to fix once drilling has started.

Proposed scope of work

Pressure regime analysis from logs, seismic and offset wells, pore pressure and fracture gradient prediction, wellbore stability and mud weight window studies, casing seat selection, sand production and compaction risk, and fault reactivation screening.

Enquire about this service
Service 05

Environmental Services

Baseline and impact studies, environmental management and compliance support that keep projects moving through regulatory approval.

The problem it addresses

Regulatory approvals, lender requirements and community expectations are now gating items on project schedules. An incomplete baseline study, an undocumented waste or spill procedure, or a weak monitoring record can stop work, attract sanctions and damage standing with the host community.

Proposed scope of work

Baseline and environmental impact studies, environmental and social management plans, permitting and regulatory liaison, waste management and disposal planning, spill response and remediation support, decommissioning and abandonment studies, and environmental monitoring and reporting.

Enquire about this service

Deliverables

  • Baseline and impact assessment study
  • Environmental and social management plan
  • Permitting and compliance register
  • Waste, spill response and remediation plans
  • Monitoring and reporting schedule

Deliverables

  • Concept selection and feasibility report
  • FEED and design support package
  • Process and flow assurance studies
  • Equipment datasheets and specification register
  • Commissioning and handover support records
Service 06

Oil and Gas Services: Facilities & Project Support

Engineering and project support for production facilities and surface infrastructure, from concept selection through execution to commissioning and handover.

The problem it addresses

Facility decisions taken late, or without input from the subsurface picture, drive cost growth, schedule slip and rework. Concept select is the cheapest place to change a design; every stage after it costs an order of magnitude more to change.

Proposed scope of work

Concept selection and feasibility studies, FEED and detailed design support, process and flow assurance studies, facilities sizing and layout studies, materials and equipment specification, vendor documentation review, commissioning and start-up support, and brownfield modification and debottlenecking studies.
