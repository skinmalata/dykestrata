About Dykestrata Geoenergy Services Ltd

Integrated geoscience and energy technical services for subsurface evaluation, petroleum exploration, reservoir characterisation, and informed energy-sector decisions.

Our Company

Geoscience insight for better energy decisions.

Dykestrata Geoenergy Services Ltd is a Nigerian geoscience and energy services company established to provide technical solutions for subsurface evaluation, petroleum exploration, reservoir characterisation, and related energy industry requirements.

We focus on transforming geological, geophysical, petrophysical, and well data into meaningful technical interpretations that support exploration planning, reservoir understanding, field development, and asset evaluation.

Our business model combines in-house technical capability, strategic partnerships, specialist software, and collaborative project execution to deliver solutions tailored to oil and gas operators, service companies, and other energy-sector stakeholders. Explore our technical services.

Our Approach

An integrated view of the subsurface.

We believe that effective subsurface evaluation requires more than isolated datasets. By integrating geology, geophysics, petrophysics, well information, and relevant engineering inputs, we aim to provide clients with a more coherent understanding of their subsurface assets.

Our delivery approach emphasises clearly defined scopes of work, appropriate quality assurance, fit-for-purpose technical workflows, and practical recommendations supported by available data.
